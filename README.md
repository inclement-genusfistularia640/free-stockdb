## 🛠 Using the Software

Upon first launch, the application creates a folder on your computer. This folder acts as your primary database.

### Syncing New Data
Select the "Update Data" button in the menu bar. The software connects to the source, identifies gaps in your records, and downloads the missing pieces. This process happens in the background. You view the progress bar to track the status.

### Running Calculations
Choose the stock symbol from your list. Select the timeframe you wish to view. Add an indicator, such as a moving average, to visualize the trend. The engine performs the computation on your local machine and displays the results in a new window.

### Backtesting Logic
Create your custom trading rules in the settings screen. You select the range of dates for your test. The application iterates through the past data to show potential results. Review the performance metrics to refine your approach.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the software require a subscription?**
No. This tool is free to use. You download, install, and manage your own database without recurring costs.

**Why does the initial sync take time?**
The software builds a library of historical data on your first run. The time depends on your internet speed and the amount of data you download. Subsequent updates only fetch the recent daily changes.

**Can I export my data?**
Yes. You export your results to standard file formats for use in other spreadsheet programs. 

**Is this tool for live trading?**
The software serves research and quantitative testing purposes. It does not connect to brokerage accounts for placing live orders. It remains a tool for analysis and strategy development.
